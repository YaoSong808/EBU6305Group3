Goal of the project: Popularize film knowledge to people

Target Users: General public

Context of Use: When people want to learn about the origin, types or other related film culture of movies, they can use our website

##
Movies are selected as the subject of an interactive multimedia website application

### 1. ** The story behind the movie **
Design a website that delve into the backstory, production, behind-the-scenes, and little-known fun facts of your chosen movie. Unique content can be provided through video interviews, production diaries, behind-the-scenes photos, and original articles. This approach can provide movie lovers with new ways to gain insight into their favorite movies.

### 2. ** Cinematic character interaction **
Create a website that gives users an interactive insight into the main characters in the movie. This can be done through an interactive timeline of character backstories, a network of character relationships, or a mini-game of character perspective. This approach can increase the user's sense of engagement with the plot and characters of the movie.

### 3. ** Virtual Movie Tour **
Virtual Reality (VR) or Augmented reality (AR) technology is used to provide users with an immersive experience of movie scenes. By simulating key locations in movies, users can "walk" through movie scenes, explore details, and even participate in certain classic scenes.

### 4. ** Movie-themed interactive education **
For the field of education, an interactive teaching platform with the theme of movies can be developed, and many fields such as history, culture, science and technology can be explored through movies. For example, historical movies are used to explain specific historical events, or sci-fi movies are used to explore scientific principles and technological innovations.

### 5. ** Community involvement **
Build a community of movie lovers, allowing users to upload their reviews, discuss forums, participate in online Q&A, and vote (e.g., favorite movie characters, most powerful movie scenes, etc.). Enhance user engagement and website stickability through community interaction.

### Technical and Design advice:
- ** Dynamic user interfaces ** : Use dynamic effects and transitions to increase the appeal of your website.
- ** Highly interactive ** : Design interactive quizzes, mini-games, or simulations to increase user engagement.
- ** Responsive design ** : Making sure your website looks good on all kinds of devices including desktop, tablet and mobile.
- ** Visual & Audio effects ** : Take advantage of high quality image, video, and audio content to provide an immersive experience for your users.
- ** Ease of use and accessibility ** : Designing clear navigation ensures that the website is easy for all users to access and use.

By combining the above ideas and technical advice, your project can not only meet the requirements of the course, but also provide unique and engaging content for your target audience.
